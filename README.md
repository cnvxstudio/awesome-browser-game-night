# Awesome Browser Game Night

A focused collection of word, drawing and guessing games, plus practical resources for a small game night in person or over a call.

Maintained by the Blather team. Blather and its companion materials are included and clearly identified below; the other projects are independent. This is a curated resource list, not a ranking, a hands-on review or an endorsement by those projects.

## Word and clue games

- [Codenames Online](https://codenames.game/) - Official online version of the board game. Its own instructions explain nickname entry, room settings, sharing the room URL and coordinating over audio or video chat.
- [JKLM.FUN](https://jklm.fun/) - Browser party-game hub including BombParty, Master of the Grid and PopSauce. It provides public and private room controls; choose the particular game and language before inviting friends.
- [Blather](https://blatherround.com/) - Our independent English word party game for 2-6 humans. A describer builds clues from limited word tiles while others type guesses; earlier guesses can become new clue material. Choose 1-5 rounds, with one describing turn per player per round.

## Drawing and guessing games

- [skribbl.io](https://skribbl.io/) - Free multiplayer drawing and guessing. One player draws a chosen word and the others guess; the official site offers private rooms and describes the scoring and round structure.
- [Gartic Phone](https://garticphone.com/) - A telephone-style sequence of writing, drawing and describing. Its official instructions recommend a voice call and finish by revealing how the sequence changed.
- [Drawasaurus](https://www.drawasaurus.org/) - Free browser drawing and guessing, with public play and private rooms for friends. Its homepage links the game rules and browser-support information.

## Voice and session planning

- [Jitsi Meet](https://jitsi.org/jitsi-meet/) - Open-source conferencing software and a link to its hosted meeting service. Useful as a separate call alongside a browser game; check the chosen service's current host sign-in requirements.
- [Blather game-night host kit](https://github.com/cnvxstudio/blather-game-night-kit) - Our public host checklist and blank recap materials. Useful for agreeing on participants, turn order and what to discuss after the game.
- [Blather Game Night Host prompt](https://github.com/cnvxstudio/blather-game-night-kit/blob/main/skills/blather-game-night-host/SKILL.md) - Our complete Markdown planning instructions for the AI assistant a host already uses. Produces an invitation, turn table, short teaching script and recap questions. It neither controls live rooms nor accesses player records.
- [Blather example demo](https://blatherround.com/demo) - Our prepared example of the word-tile clue interaction. This is a learning example, separate from a live multiplayer session.

## Choose a format

| Your group wants to... | Start by exploring | What to arrange first |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Build clues with constrained words | Blather | 2-6 humans, English words, 1-5 rounds |
| Play the Codenames board game online | Codenames Online | Room settings and a shared call |
| Draw a word for friends to guess | skribbl.io or Drawasaurus | A private room and drawing devices |
| Watch a sentence change through drawings | Gartic Phone | A group, a call and a chosen mode |
| Explore several room-based party games | JKLM.FUN | A particular game and language |

These are format suggestions based on the linked official descriptions, not quality scores. Player limits, account requirements, modes and prices can change; consult each official page before organizing a session. The Blather limits above are specific to Blather, not the other games.

## A host checklist

1. Choose one format and confirm everyone can use its language and input method.
2. Open a private room when the selected game offers one. Share its real invitation in your existing group chat; never share a guessed room URL.
3. Test the call and the game on the devices the group will actually use. A game link and a voice-call link can be different.
4. Explain one example before the first scored round. Agree who starts, how turns rotate and when to stop.
5. For Blather, calculate turns as players multiplied by rounds: 4 players and 2 rounds means 8 describing turns. Do not apply that formula to other games automatically.
6. Before sharing a screenshot or clip, review player names and messages with the group.

## Selection and sourcing

The collection focuses on official game pages with identifiable word/drawing/guessing mechanics and resources that help a host run a small session. Descriptions were checked against those public pages on 2026-11-03. This does not claim a full multiplayer test or independent security audit of every project. No affiliate links, paid placements or reciprocal-link requirement are used in this list.

## Contributing

Suggest an official project URL and a short factual description through a GitHub issue or pull request. Explain which group format it supports. Disclose any association with the project. Avoid referral links, invented player experiences, star ratings, duplicate entries and copied promotional claims. For corrections, identify the entry and link the current official information.
